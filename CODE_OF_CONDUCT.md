# Code of conduct

BinaryHunter contributors and users are expected to keep project spaces respectful,
technical and safe.

- Be constructive and discuss the work rather than the person.
- Respect privacy, ownership and legal restrictions around ECU firmware and data.
- Do not publish customer files, credentials, VINs or proprietary definitions.
- Do not harass, threaten or discriminate against other participants.
- Accept reasonable moderation intended to protect the project and its users.

Unacceptable conduct may be removed and repeated or serious violations may result in
restricted participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner.
